## You can, without asking

- **Say what you use.** "This repo uses docs-kit." "Checked with govkit."
- **Link to the kit**, and quote its README or docs with a link back.
- **Name the kit in an article, a talk or a review.**
- **Keep the names inside the code and its `NOTICE` file**, as the license asks.

## Ask first

- **Name a fork or a changed copy with a kit's name**, or with "Techsilon". A fork uses its own name. It may say "based on docs-kit by Techsilon LLC".
- **Use a name in a product, a company or a domain name.**
- **Say or suggest that Techsilon LLC made, checked or endorses your product.**
